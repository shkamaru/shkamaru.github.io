Recipe Finder searches YouTube for cooking videos and uses Claude to extract clean, structured ingredient lists and step-by-step methods from each result. It intelligently routes between video descriptions and transcripts to minimize API costs. Built mobile-first with Next.js.

Inspired by the everyday frustration of scrolling through recipe videos only to realize you're missing half the ingredients.
