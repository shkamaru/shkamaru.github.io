A fast-paced party game where players get their team to guess iconic Indian personalities—from film, cricket, politics, business, and more—without saying their name or using obvious clues. Perfect for family gatherings and house parties, it sparks nostalgia, laughter, and hilarious hints for desi audiences of all ages.

Born out of our own social gatherings where we wanted to do something more than playing Poker to involve everyone for a fun time.
